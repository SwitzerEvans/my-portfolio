About Me

Hi, I'm Chiemeka Evans, a passionate front-end web developer who enjoys transforming creative ideas into interactive websites. My journey into web development started with curiosity. I wanted to understand how the websites I visited every day were built. That curiosity grew into a passion, and I've been learning and practicing ever since.

I'm currently focusing on mastering HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which are the core building blocks of the web. I love working on projects that challenge me to solve problems, improve my design sense, and make websites that are not only functional but also visually appealing.

Beyond coding, I'm deeply interested in UI/UX design because I believe that good design creates better experiences for users. My goal is to bridge the gap between design and development — creating websites that look great and feel smooth to use.

When I'm not coding, I spend time researching modern web trends, practicing small projects like to-do lists, landing pages, and portfolio templates, and sharing my progress online. I strongly believe in continuous learning, and my long-term dream is to become a full-stack developer and eventually build my own digital products.
